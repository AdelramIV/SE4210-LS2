# Reflections
This was a quick experimental dive into using AI to assist with learning about arithmetic overflow. I felt it was a good exercise to gain a bit more familiarity with using AI tools and to better understand arithmetic overflow.

I think Claude actually did a fairly nice job creating a quick HTML page that demonstrates well how arithmetic overflow can be triggered, giving me both signed and unsigned examples.

The interactive elements were quite well done, making it easy to see what sorts of combinations could lead to overflow events and giving me a good sense of what not to do in the future when creating any script involving arithmetic.

## Workflow
To quickly go through how this came to be, it was a quick one-prompt generation in Claude asking it to put together a one-page HTML script that visually demonstrates arithmetic overflow with interactive elements. Luckily, the first generation seemed to come out alright, and it was a cakewalk from there.
